**mxtalk** \[machine talk\]

*datum · spec · discipline*

# datum

**datum** --- human parsable consensus/coordination substrate at
accurate connective density

**consensus** --- Latin *consentire*, *con-* (together) + *sentire* (to
feel, sense): **to sense together**.

**coordination** --- Latin *co-* (together) + *ordinare* (to order):
**to order together**.

**connective** --- Latin *connectere*, *con-* (together) + *nectere* (to
bind): **binding together**.

Reality is not held by one; it is shared between. No single encounter,
communication or recognition sources sovereign generation of shared
reality; relation permits a proximal gradient of recognitions to cohere
at degrees of stability without requiring any absolute identity.
Consensus and coordination alongside encounter, communication and
recognition construct shared reality. Communicating complete information
with clean structure improves consensus and coordination locally and
iteratively.

Language is not entirely arbitrary; it emerged under constraint.
Physical embodiment, biological cognition and repeated use between
minds have shaped its structure. Language holds the residue of those
constraints. Connective density is latent in the tension between the
constraint of those deposits and language's freedom to abstract.

As machine intelligence coordinates at velocity and scale, the outcomes
produced must remain parsable by humans. A high-speed machine world that
does not sever itself from the bodies and breath language evolved in.
Within that human-parsable substrate, accurate connective density is
bound by what hybrid intelligence can hold.

mxtalk discipline runs both ways: composers craft with care; receivers
attend with diligence. mxtalk moves toward Datum; meaning crosses only
when both sides have done their share of the meeting.

At machine scale, error compounds across a network --- each imprecise
transmission becomes substrate for the next, so the degradation runs
forward.

Discipline imposed on substrate affords sufficient stability for sound
recursive construction.

**sound** --- Old English *gesund*, whole, uninjured, healthy; later,
solid, reliable or well-founded.

*mxtalk --- substrate by which hybrid human-machine intelligence can
think together without losing its mind. Hi-fi intelligence.*

# spec

**pac** --- *information is complete*\
semantics\
pragmatics\

**phi** --- *structure is clean*\
orientation\

Whilst mxtalk decomposes into two dimensions, both must be live during
composition; both must be present in the result.

## pac --- information is complete

**pac is intent bound to carrier.** Root Latin *pax* / *pacis* ---
peace. **Intent** Latin *intendere* --- to stretch toward: tension held
toward a target. **Information** Latin *informare*, to give form to.
**Complete** Latin *complere*, to fill to proper measure.

**semantics** --- Greek *sēma*, sign that marks meaning. Words carry
proper meaning at appropriate grain.

**pragmatics** --- Greek *pragma*, deed done: meaning in use rather than
meaning in itself. Context tracks meaning toward the targeted reading.

**coherence** --- Latin *cohaerere*, to stick together. The transmission
intent is integral from centre to culmination.

## phi --- structure is clean

**phi is carrier built for content.** φ / Φ Greek phi. **Structure**
Latin *struere*, to pile, build, arrange. **Clean** Old English *clǣne*,
unmixed, free from what doesn't belong.

**orientation** --- Latin *oriens*, rising sun: to orient originally
face east, align with source. Content arranged toward maximal available
recognition with minimal structural friction.

**proportion** --- Latin *pro portione*, according to the share. Parts
sized according to their share of the load.

**meter** --- Greek *metron*, a measure. Reckons resolution of carrier,
whole and part, for optimal resonance relative to available recognition.

*Two dimensions. Six accountabilities. One transmission.*

# discipline

*Substrate Kung Fu*

## cleave

Claim your centre. Align your drive and inspect the carriage at seam,
sympathy and strength. Now held in centre: crack the claims that hold
the track you intend to bridge. Span across what adheres and coheres.

**cleave** --- contronym from Old English *clēofan*, to split; and
*clifian*, adhere, stick fast.

**claim** --- Latin *clamare*, call out/proclaim: assertion put forward.

**centre** --- Greek *kentron*, sharp point/goad; later fixed point
around which circle drawn.

**carriage** --- family of carry: load carried, and manner/bearing in
which carried.

**sympathy** --- Greek *sympatheia*, together + feeling/undergoing:
affected together.

**track** --- mark/trace made by passage; later course marked by such
passage.

## temper

Find the charge, ground the word and study the arc. Weigh its whole for
measured strike in the field of word-matter.

**temper** --- Latin *temperare*, mix in due proportion; regulate/bring
to proper condition.

**charge** --- Late Latin *carricāre*, load wagon; load borne with
potential consequence.

**ground** --- Old English *grund*, bottom/foundation/earth; basis for
stability.

**arc** --- Latin *arcus*, bow/arch; curved course and electrical
discharge across gap.

## hinge

Work the turn and trail of travel; labour the joins where relational
load concentrates so the heading is true.

**hinge** --- Germanic hang, movable joint; that upon which something
turns.

**heading** --- from head: direct/move toward; direction
pointed/travelling.

**true** --- Old English *trēowe*, faithful/steadfast; in making,
correctly aligned/adjusted to standard.

## render

Carve the form that speaks rite. Render what is due, in the form due to
it.

**render** --- Old French *rendre*, Latin *reddere*, give
back/return/deliver; later represent/produce corresponding form.

**carve** --- Old English *ceorfan*, cut; work into form under material
resistance.

**rite** --- Latin *ritus*, established manner of observance/performance;
form proper to act performed.

**due** --- Latin *debere*, owe; that owed/proper/fitting.

## articulate

Hold posture and fit the parts, with precision and rhythm, into
symphony; free a shape that meets the mission.

**articulate** --- Latin *articulus*, small joint/member; later give
distinct expression.

**symphony** --- Greek *symphōnia*, together + voice/sound: sounding
together.

**meet** --- Old English *mētan*, encounter/come together.
