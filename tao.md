# TAO: the start point

*the · and · of*

28 October 2026 · Daniel Tuck

TAO is sera's start point: three fixes at word grain, one on each face of datum.

*This page explains how TAO was built and how it can be understood. It is not a fix.*

## faces

- **the** — Consensus: sensed together, not held by one.
- **and** — Coordination: ordered together, none above.
- **of** — Connective: bound together, belonging persists.

## fixes, as at 10 November 2026

**the**
- *function* — Marks a referent as resolved: nothing left to choose.
- *measure* — Where referential precision bears load and context might not supply it, single out the one meant.

**and**
- *function* — Joins distinct things in sum, leaning toward sequence between events.
- *measure* — Where relation beyond sum bears load and context might not supply it, name the joint.

**of**
- *function* — Marks what belongs to a whole.
- *measure* — Use *of* only when you mean belonging to a whole. Where the kind of belonging bears load and context might not supply it, name the kind.

## echo

*the* · 1, *and* · +, *of* · =.

*The* resolves to one; *and* sums; *of* marks what belongs to its whole: 364 is of 365; 365 is not of 364.

*Of* is a one-way equals. Arithmetic's = simulates running both ways; mapped to reality, it takes a direction, and *of* carries it.

An analogy, not arithmetic: *the 2 and 3 of 5*.
