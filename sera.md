# sera

**sera** — Stratified Encounters and Recognitions Archive.

28 October 2026 · Daniel Tuck

## commons

sera is a commons: an open record of how meaning crosses between intelligence.

## datum

Datum is defined in mxtalk.md: fixed, not immovable.

## gravitas

Fixes gravitate toward datum as they grow gravitas. Each one builds datum further from principle into practice; datum as defined in mxtalk.md stays intact.

Gravitas grows as consensus and coordination cohere through a fix. It is continuous and calculated from evidence. Evidence comes from survey passes, and from tests of the repairs they log.

A fix must be encounterable: a position held sufficiently stable to be found again, tested and related.

**fix** — A position on datum.

**survey** — Fixes set against text.

**pass** - One surveyor's run through a survey.

**repair** — A change logged against the original text, where a fix calls for it.

## strata

Recognitions cohere at degrees of stability. Strata can build out those degrees, if that proves useful.

## TAO

TAO is the start point, to be developed into a firm base. The work builds from there.

Its three fixes are *the*, *and* and *of*. Each holds two fields, function and measure, and is tested, recognized and revised on its own.
